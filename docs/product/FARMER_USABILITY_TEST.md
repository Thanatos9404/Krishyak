# Farmer usability protocol
Status: prepared; no real farmer participants tested or results fabricated. Browser automation is engineering verification and cannot substitute for farmer research.

Recruit 5–8 consenting farmers with varied literacy/languages and Android devices, ideally with an FPO facilitator. Record no names, precise farm locations, health data or phone numbers in public notes. Use synthetic fields, explain withdrawal and obtain research consent separately from product consent.

Tasks: explain today's first suggested inspection; locate its evidence/date/limit; add a manually measured field; record an irrigation or field note; describe an unavailable scan; compare place/date market prices; turn on private-device offline storage and recover a queued note; find export/sign-out. Do not ask participants to act on agronomic advice.

Capture task completion, time, errors, hesitation, comprehension, language preference and assistance; allow think-aloud without coaching. Test 320/360px devices, 200% text/zoom, intermittent connectivity, keyboard or screen reader if applicable. Success target: at least 80% unassisted completion on core tasks and no safety-critical misunderstanding. Treat this as a prospective target, not an achieved metric. Prioritize terminology/source comprehension before adding more panels. Human translation and agronomic review are required before claiming local-language field readiness.
