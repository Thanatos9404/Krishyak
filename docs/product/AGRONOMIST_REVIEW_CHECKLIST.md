# Agronomist review checklist
Status: not independently reviewed. Release review must be completed by an accountable agronomist; do not infer endorsement from browser tests.

Review each action's trigger, conclusion, urgency, why, source date and missing-data behavior. Confirm the system never equates an index with disease, fertility, yield or a universal health score. Examine NDVI/NDMI/NDRE interpretation across crop stages, boundary revisions, cloudy/missing observations and providers. Confirm soil units and provenance distinguish reported lab/soil-card data from estimates.

Check photo-supported crops, unsupported abstention, score language, external evaluation limits and suggestions requiring field confirmation. Verify no pesticide recommendation, diagnosis or treatment instruction is hidden behind confidence. Review weather source/time/horizon and simulated versus measured results. Review irrigation/fertilizer/outcome units and actual versus projected costs/yields.

Verify market date/variety/unit/place, MSP reference limits and benefits' official sources/eligibility disclaimers. Confirm consent withdrawal stops future research linkage and institution views remain role-scoped. Record reviewer identity privately, review date, versions, objections and resolved changes; publish only authorized conclusions. Any unsafe interpretation blocks promotion.
