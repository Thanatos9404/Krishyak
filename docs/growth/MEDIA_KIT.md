# Krishyak media kit
## Factual description
Krishyak is an open-source farm decision and field-evidence companion. The rebuilt farmer workflow joins field records, observations, crop photos, weather context, satellite provenance where configured, reported market data and scenario planning. Its interactive public demonstration is synthetic.

Creator: Yashvardhan Thanvi, verified from the public Thanatos9404 GitHub profile. The public source is https://github.com/Thanatos9404/Krishyak. Do not describe stock-photography subjects as team members or customers.

## What can be demonstrated
Five-tab farmer flow, optional field boundary/manual area, evidence provenance, private photo history, consent controls, queued observations, account export, preserved simulation inputs, separate role-based pilot review. A reproducible engineering and model-governance record is available in the repository.

## Claims and limitations
No verified farmer adoption or yield/profit impact claim is supplied. No government endorsement, scheme enrollment guarantee or universal disease diagnosis is claimed. The published classifier's internal accuracy was 93.71% on 8566 examples; external accuracy was 57.21% on 229 examples, confidence interval 50.73–63.44%. These are evaluation conditions, not calibrated individual disease probabilities or field effectiveness.

Live satellite, SMS, storage and durable infrastructure depend on deployment configuration. Legal/agronomic review and real farmer/device research are explicit external gates. Demo screenshots must retain synthetic labels.
