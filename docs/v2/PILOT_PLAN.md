# Consented farmer pilot protocol

No completed pilot or measured agronomic impact is claimed. The application
supports consented selected-plot enrollment, source-labelled timelines, actions,
yield/irrigation/treatment/expense/income reports, feedback and private review.
Only the cohort owner can view institutional reports. Distributions are
suppressed below five currently consented farmers. Individual IDs, phones,
polygons, photos and free-text notes are not included in those reports.

Before enrollment, appoint an accountable operator/agronomist, approve language
and privacy notices, validate production services/provider terms, establish an
incident channel, and obtain separate pilot/research opt-ins. Begin in one
district with one or two supported crops; target 25–50 consenting farmers for
feasibility rather than claiming statistical proof from that count.

Record baseline field/crop/stage, independent soil/inspection provenance,
irrigation history, dates and units. Predefine weather/index alert thresholds and
expert inspection workflow. Compare satellite masks/index intervals to reference
imagery and actual inspection; report cloud/missingness separately. Disease
evaluation needs independent expert labels and grouped holdouts, not in-app
agreement rates. Never direct automatic pesticide doses or irrigation.

Capture usability, consent withdrawals, evidence completeness, provider failures,
offline queue outcomes and optional expert review. Economic/yield claims require
verified harvest area/mass and receipts, a defensible comparison design,
predeclared exclusions and uncertainty. The API keeps improvement fields null.

Pilot report: dates/district/crops; recruited/enrolled/withdrawn counts; consent
versions; device/language mix; data sources/missingness; alert precision and
inspection delay with definitions; independent model coverage/accuracy;
verified outcomes with method/uncertainty; incidents/costs; operator sign-off.
Farmer-entered records alone do not establish impact or causal improvement.
